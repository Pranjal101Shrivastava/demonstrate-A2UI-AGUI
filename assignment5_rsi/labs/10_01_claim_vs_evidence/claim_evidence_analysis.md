# Claim vs evidence: Budget RSI (arXiv:2610.29121)

Source: the course's [paper summary](https://github.com/dlmastery/simple-coding-harness/blob/main/12_october_2026_papers/PAPER_SUMMARIES.md)
and lab 10.01 page. Laptop column = our SignalForge adaptation ([`signalforge/methods/budget_rsi.py`](../../signalforge/methods/budget_rsi.py)).

| # | Claim | Supporting experiment | Conditions that limit it | Laptop adaptation preserves | Laptop adaptation changes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Scoring code edits before they run reaches 87.0% held-out on HumanEval+" | GPT-4o scaffold search, 48/164 steering split, HGM selection, readout fit on 96 edits × 16-instance fit split | one base model, one benchmark family, one selector, one budget | fit/steering/held-out protocol; 96 labelled edits; HGM Thompson selection; earliest-max return rule | edits are generated by operators, not an LLM; the benchmark is traffic control; 48/16/100 instance splits |
| 2 | The readout beats the **parent-rate ablation** (87.0 vs 84.2) | same search with a readout that only sees parent solve rate | a 2.8-point gap; variance across runs is not in the summary | the ablation exactly (`mode="parent_rate"`) | we measure +2.9 ± ~10 over 8 seeds (lab 10.03): the same direction, and also noisy |
| 3 | Scoring saves most of the evaluation cost ("scores 100+ edits in seconds", "~88% reduction") | counting evaluations: 128 × 100 full vs 96 × 16 labels | the course's 88% leaves out the steering evaluations of *expanded* nodes | cost ledger per purpose (`readout-labels`, `expansion`) | we count everything: 78% saving vs evaluating all 8 children of each expansion (lab 10.03 step 5) |

**Untested by the paper (per the summary), and what would test it:**
* *Other base models / proposal distributions.* Lab 10.04 shows why this matters: a readout trained on edits from a different
  parent distribution ("shifted scaffold") ranks the parents HGM actually expands worse, and the search loses ~12 held-out instances.
* *Features beyond the generic 8.* Lab 10.05: task-specific features help **only** together with online refitting.
* *Longer budgets.* Lab 10.18 runs two budgets (2,400 and 4,800 units).
